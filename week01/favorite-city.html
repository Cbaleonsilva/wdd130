My Favorite City

My favorite city is Cholula, in Puebla, Mexico, is one of Mesoamerica's oldest continuously inhabited cities and my absolute favorite place. Famous for its breathtaking view of the Popocatépetl volcano, it is home to the Great Pyramid of Cholula (Tlachihualtepetl), which boasts the largest base of any pyramid in the world and is crowned by the iconic Nuestra Señora de los Remedios sanctuary. This vibrant Magical Town (Pueblo Mágico) perfectly blends ancient indigenous traditions, colorful colonial architecture, and a lively university atmosphere, making it a truly magical city to explore.
